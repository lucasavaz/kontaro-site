FAQ

Questions teams ask first.

Do I keep my own WhatsApp number?

Yes. You connect your own WhatsApp Business number through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform. The number stays yours.

Which AI model does the assistant use?

The one you choose. You connect your own AI provider, so you decide the model and keep the account with that provider.

Can the assistant reply with voice?

Yes. You choose whether replies go out as text or as voice notes.

What happens when the AI can't answer?

It hands the conversation to a person on your team with the full history, so the customer doesn't have to repeat anything.

Can I connect my own systems?

Yes. The Kontaro API lets other systems send messages through your workspace, and Data Sources can read spreadsheets from Google Drive.
